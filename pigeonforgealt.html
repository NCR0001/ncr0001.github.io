About Pigeon Forge

Pigeon Forge is a vibrant mountain town known for its entertainment, shopping, and hospitality. Nestled near the Smoky Mountains, it blends fun, family attractions with Southern charm and culture.

Attractions

From Dollywood theme park to comedy shows and mini golf, Pigeon Forge offers endless fun for visitors of all ages. It's a hub for entertainment and excitement in the Smokies.

Restaurants

Enjoy hearty Southern meals, classic diners, and international cuisine throughout Pigeon Forge. There’s something for every taste and budget.

Lodging

Whether you're looking for luxurious resorts, rustic cabins, or budget-friendly hotels, Pigeon Forge has a wide range of places to stay, all with great access to attractions.
