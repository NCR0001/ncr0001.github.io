About Pigeon Forge

Pigeon Forge is a lively town in the Smoky Mountains known for its family-friendly attractions, thrilling entertainment, and southern hospitality. It’s home to Dollywood, countless shows, and easy access to the natural beauty of the Great Smoky Mountains National Park.
